Featured Case Study

From Excel Gymnastics to Lean Workflow

Business Process Re-engineering and Architectural Excellence at Retta Property Management

The Challenge

Retta Property Management faced a critical bottleneck: 25%+ of property manager time was consumed by manual invoice re-billing processes. This repetitive work prevented skilled professionals from focusing on high-value strategic tasks.

The Solution

Zeal AI implemented an agentic AI-powered automation system using cutting-edge technology:

  • Tech Stack: TypeScript, Vercel (serverless), Firebase Firestore (NoSQL), Google Gemini 2.5 Pro
  • Methodology: Forward Deployment Engineering with 100% satisfaction money-back guarantee
  • Architecture: Custom-built AI agent with optimized 1M token context window, parallel processing
  • Approach: Human-in-the-loop verification with generation-validation cycles

The Results

78% Cost Reduction
10x Faster Processing
99.9% Accuracy
2 min Response Time

Key Innovations

  • Serverless, SQL-less architecture for maximum scalability and zero infrastructure management
  • Intelligent product matching across naming variations using natural language understanding
  • Automated price validation against complex business rules
  • Context-aware billing justification generation
  • Real-time collaborative validation UI with transparent AI reasoning
  • Continuous improvement loops with systematic feedback collection
